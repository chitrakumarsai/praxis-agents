### Define success before building

- Write acceptance criteria before choosing models or prompts: required outcomes, evidence,
  prohibited effects, and cost/latency limits.
- Never target an undefined "high accuracy". Name the scored unit, the eligible population,
  the denominator, and the severity of each kind of mistake.
- Keep mandatory constraints as separate pass/fail gates; never average them into a quality score.
- Collect reviewed examples early, including hard, missing-data, conflicting, and should-abstain cases.
