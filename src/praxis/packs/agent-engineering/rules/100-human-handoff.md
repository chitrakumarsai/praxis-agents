### Make human involvement explicit

- Define escalation triggers up front: missing authorization, materially ambiguous goals,
  consequential actions, conflicting evidence, exhausted recovery.
- Bind approval to the specific action and parameters; recheck if they change. Silence, timeout,
  or an agent-generated approval is not consent. Don't re-ask for approval already in scope.
- A handoff includes the proposed action, evidence, completed work, uncertainty, and how execution resumes.
- Model self-reported confidence alone is not a routing rule.
