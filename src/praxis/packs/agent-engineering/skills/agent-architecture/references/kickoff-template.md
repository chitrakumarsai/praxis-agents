# AI product kickoff template

Copy into a project brief and replace every placeholder with a decision, owner, or measurement.

```markdown
# <Project name> — AI Product Kickoff

## Problem and users
- User and task trigger:
- Current workflow and measured pain:
- Desired result and user benefit:
- Scope and non-goals:

## Success criteria
- Required outcomes and verification evidence:
- Unacceptable mistakes and prohibited effects:
- Human review expectations:
- Quality, cost, latency, and reliability targets:
- Metric populations, denominators, and owners:

## Feasibility
- Available data, tools, permissions, and constraints:
- Most consequential uncertainties:
- Experiments, time limits, and proceed/revise/stop criteria:

## Initial architecture
- Simplest baseline:
- Workflow pattern and why it is needed:
- Model responsibilities versus deterministic code:
- State, context sources, and memory policy:
- Tool contracts and external side effects:

## Minimum harness
- Validators, authorization, and execution isolation:
- Retry, time, cost, and iteration limits:
- Completion, no-progress, failure, and cancellation rules:
- Checkpoints and uncertain-write reconciliation:
- Human approval and escalation rules:
- Tracing, sensitive-data handling, and retention:

## Evaluation
- Representative tasks and reviewed expected outcomes:
- Development, held-out, capability, and regression coverage:
- Graders, calibration, trial policy, and uncertainty reporting:
- Baseline results and known limitations:

## Team and delivery
- Product/domain, AI, platform, evaluation, and release owners:
- First complete slice and exit criteria:
- Next experiment and decision date:
- Pilot scope, monitoring, rollback criteria, and owner:
- Production feedback and dataset maintenance process:
```
